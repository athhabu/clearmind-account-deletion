ClearMind Account Deletion

This page explains how ClearMind users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your ClearMind account, send an email to:

aaalabs.support@gmail.com

Request Account Deletion

Please send the request from the email address associated with your ClearMind account. This helps us verify the account ownership before processing the request.

What will be deleted?

When an account deletion request is processed, ClearMind will delete the applicable account and associated personal data maintained by ClearMind, subject to any data that must be retained for legitimate legal, security, or operational reasons.

This may include:

  • Your ClearMind account
  • Your email address associated with the account
  • Authentication-related account information
  • Other personal information associated with your account that ClearMind maintains

Data that may not be deleted immediately

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

ClearMind does not currently permanently store Brain Dumps in its Supabase database. Brain Dump text may be transmitted to third-party AI services for processing as described in the ClearMind Privacy Policy.

Processing your request

After receiving your request, we may need to verify that you are the owner of the account. Once verified, we will process the deletion request and remove applicable account data.

Contact

If you have questions about account or data deletion, contact:

aaalabs.support@gmail.com
